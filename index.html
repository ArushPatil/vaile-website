STUDIO PROTOCOL • FAQ

Key guidance regarding sizing, canvas patina, and allocation.

01 • How does the Edition 01 silhouette fit? +

Edition 01 features a mid-high rise with an architectural relaxed taper and ergonomic knee articulation. Select your true waist measurement (28 to 36). If between sizes, we recommend sizing up for an effortless tailored drape.

02 • How should 12 oz heavyweight duck canvas be washed? +

Wash inside-out in cold water using a mild detergent on gentle cycle, then hang dry away from direct sunlight. Avoid machine drying to allow the raw cotton fibers and mineral dyes to wear in naturally with a personalized patina.

03 • How does the numbered allocation of 50 work? +

Each release is strictly limited to 50 individually numbered pairs, hand-stamped on the inner studio archive patch (e.g. 01/50). Once batch allocations are exhausted, the archive run closes.

04 • How does direct WhatsApp allocation work? +

Tapping 'Request Allocation' opens a direct conversation with our Bangalore atelier on WhatsApp (+91 8951066881). We verify sizing, confirm transit dispatch details, and handle your order directly with white-glove care.

05 • What is covered under Lifetime Studio Repair? +

We provide complimentary lifetime repair for all structural seam blowouts, hem adjustments, and solid alloy hardware replacements through our Bangalore studio for the life of your garment.
