FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01 HOW DOES THE EDITION 01 ARCHITECTURAL SILHOUETTE FIT? +

Edition 01 features a high-rise waist with a structured, relaxed straight leg and engineered knee volume articulation. Select your true waist measurement (28 to 36). For a more exaggerated, oversized architectural drape, we recommend ordering one size up.

02 HOW DOES 12 OZ DUCK CANVAS BREAK IN OVER TIME? +

Our raw 405 GSM cotton duck canvas arrives crisp and structural, standing clean over footwear. With regular friction and movement, high-wear zones (knees, pocket openings, seams) soften naturally, developing unique marbled fades and personalized creasing.

03 HOW ARE NUMBERED ALLOCATIONS ASSIGNED? +

Production of this pant is strictly capped at 50 individually numbered garments, hand-stamped onto the inner studio archive patch (e.g. 07/50). Numbers are assigned sequentially in order of confirmation. Once 50 units are allocated, the edition is permanently archived.
