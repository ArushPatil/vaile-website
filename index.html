FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

01 HOW DOES THE EDITION 01 ARCHITECTURAL SILHOUETTE FIT? +

Edition 01 features a mid-high rise with an architectural relaxed taper and dual ergonomic knee articulation darts. Select your true waist measurement (28 to 36). If between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a fluid, sculptural drape over footwear.

02 HOW DOES 12 OZ DUCK CANVAS AGE & DEVELOP A PATINA? +

Our raw 405 GSM cotton duck canvas starts firm and structural, holding its form cleanly over boots. With daily wear and friction, high-contact zones (knees, pocket hems, seams) break in naturally, developing unique marbled fades and personal contouring.

03 HOW DOES THE NUMBERED ALLOCATION OF 50 WORK? +

Every release is strictly capped at 50 individually numbered units, stamped on the internal studio archive patch (e.g. 01/50). Once the 50 allocations in a batch are claimed, the archival run is closed.
