# Vaile Duck Canvas Drop — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Quarry Specimen

**Very Brief Intro:** A material-led fashion editorial that treats the pants as a field specimen: dark canvas, limestone dust, copper hardware, and measured museum-label typography. It feels intimate, weathered, and deliberate rather than commercial.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Motor Archive

**Very Brief Intro:** A hard-edged catalogue language that borrows from early utility manuals, workshop decals, and industrial inspection cards. The experience is brisk, technical, and monochrome with decisive red markings.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Soft Terrain

**Very Brief Intro:** An atmospheric outdoor campaign framed through pale sky, blurred grass, and gently oversized typography. The tone is softer and more romantic, allowing the black workwear silhouette to hold the visual weight.

**Probability:** 0.02

## Chosen Approach: Quarry Specimen

### Design Movement

**Brutalist editorial restraint** meets an analogue material archive. The site will have the quiet confidence of an independent fashion journal, using object studies, oversized type, contact-sheet framing, and archival notation rather than conventional product-card commerce.

### Core Principles

1. **Material before marketing:** The weave, weight, wear, construction, and hardware should be visually and verbally foregrounded.
2. **Asymmetric observation:** Editorial panels, oversized numerals, vertical labels, and cropped imagery create a sense of considered discovery rather than a centered sales funnel.
3. **Scarcity with calm:** “50 pieces” is stated plainly and repeatedly as a factual edition mark, without countdown pressure or artificial urgency.
4. **Tactile contrast:** Bone paper and weathered concrete counterbalance the ink-black canvas, while oxidized copper signals construction quality.

### Color Philosophy

The palette is drawn from the supplied campaign photography: **carbon black** as the garment and authority color, **limestone** as a breathing ground, **oat paper** as a tactile neutral, and **verdigris copper** as a small punctuation that leads allocation moments. The site should feel sun-faded and tactile, not digitally pristine. The signature brand color is **Oxide Copper — #A85A37**.

### Layout Paradigm

The page follows a **field-book sequence** rather than a centralized sales page. A full-bleed campaign opening moves into a slim index rail, then alternates between image-dominant spreads, narrow text columns, object labels, and an allocation ledger. Off-center alignment and intentional crop changes make the vertical scroll feel like turning through a garment study.

### Signature Elements

1. **Specimen tabs:** Tiny numbered labels and edition markers that hang from images and sections like archive tags.
2. **Stitch lines:** Low-contrast dashed dividers and bar-tack marks that echo reinforced seams.
3. **Tactile surfaces:** A paper fibre section, canvas-grain overlay, copper hardware still life, and controlled film-grain noise.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should be physical and restrained. The allocation call-to-action reads as a stamped instruction; hover states reveal a line of copper or a subtle image crop shift. Navigation scrolls to labelled sections rather than redirecting viewers away from the editorial narrative.

### Animation

At entry, copy rises a few pixels and resolves from soft opacity with a 60–90ms stagger. Scrolling uses gentle image translations, the reveal of horizontal rule segments, and parallax-like movement limited to transform and opacity. Image motion stays slow and grounded. Buttons compress slightly on press. All non-essential motion is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Bodoni Moda** provides the high-contrast editorial display voice for key declarations, in a mix of regular and italic. **DM Mono** serves as the construction language for labels, edition data, sizing, and buttons. The hierarchy is built from extreme scale contrast: headline type can exceed 120px on desktop, while specifications remain condensed and small without becoming ornamental.

### Brand Essence

**A 50-piece, private-allocation workwear study for people who treat utility as a personal uniform.**

Personality adjectives: **tactile, self-possessed, exacting.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and spare. CTAs are phrased as instructions, not generic conversion prompts. Microcopy should feel like the note on an archived sample card.

Example headline: “BUILT FOR THE WEAR YOU HAVEN’T MET YET.”

Example CTA: “REQUEST YOUR ALLOCATION / WHATSAPP”.

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses intentionally spaced mono lettering, **VAILE**, paired with the supplied radial black star emblem. The mark appears in the header, as the favicon, and as an edition seal.

## Style Decisions

1. Product photography remains the primary proof of the garment; generated art is used only for supporting texture and material atmosphere.
2. The private allocation mechanism is a WhatsApp link with a prefilled, editable enquiry; no fake cart, stock ticker, reviews, countdown, or checkout UI is introduced.
3. The experience moves between black canvas and light paper surfaces to preserve contrast and visual rhythm.
4. Motion remains restrained and local: text, navigation, and small interface moments reveal softly without shifting entire page sections.
5. The allocation experience states the price clearly and gives buyers a useful fit and care reference without inventing unconfirmed garment measurements.
6. In the absence of final campaign assets, the gallery is presented as an intentional unexposed contact-sheet archive with crop plates, inspection grids, and edition data—never as a generic placeholder.
7. Allocation calls-to-action use concise archive instructions, such as “REQUEST ALLOCATION / WHATSAPP,” rather than conventional ecommerce language.
